Every module is built on real workshop cases. Here's one example — a diagnosis that saved a customer from a major engine rebuild quote.

Case #001 · EA888 Gen3

2016 VW Golf GTI MK7 — Burning 1L oil / 1000km

Mileage: 160,000 km · Symptoms: blue smoke + oil-fouled plugs
“I'm burning a liter of oil every 1000km. Blue smoke when I start it cold, and sometimes after it's been sitting for a few minutes.”

The trap: everyone reaches for the PCV on the EA888. We replaced it. Confirmed it was good. The consumption continued.

The $0 test that solved it: idle, shut down, wait 15 minutes, restart. Big blue puff = oil pooling past worn valve stem seals. While in the top end, the timing chain tensioner was at 5/7 notches — so we did the chain at the same time. One teardown instead of two.
